# Asset attribution

The Parthenope University marks in `docs/assets/` are official assets downloaded without modification from the university's official download page:

- [Parthenope University logo download (SVG, PNG, PDF)](https://disaq.uniparthenope.it/en/download-area-disaq-materials/)
- [University visual identity page](https://www.uniparthenope.it/Portale-Ateneo/Identita_visiva)

Files used by the site: `Parthenope_bianco.svg` (white full logo) and `Pittogramma_Parthenope_Blue.svg` (blue symbol). The full logo is displayed on the dark homepage panel; the blue symbol is used as the favicon. Both files are included as downloaded and are not recolored, redrawn, or cropped.
